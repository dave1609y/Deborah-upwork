Process

How a project runs

Four stages, start to finish. Nothing waits on a shoot day or a shipping label.

1

Brief & photos

You send product photos, a short brief on the audience, and anything the ad needs to say or avoid.

2

Script & storyboard

The hook, structure, and pacing are written first — a background in mass communication and journalism shapes this stage, not just the visuals.

3

AI generation

Veo3, Kling, Runway, Higgsfield, Seedance, Nano Banana, and Midjourney build the visuals and avatar performance; ElevenLabs handles the voice.

4

Edit & deliver

CapCut brings pacing, captions, and sound together, and you get the final cut in your platform's aspect ratio, with revisions as agreed.

FAQ

Questions before you reach out

The things most brands ask before their first project. Anything else — just message directly.

What is an AI UGC ad, exactly?

A short, review-style or talking-head video ad made entirely with AI generation tools instead of a hired actor and camera crew. It's built to look and feel like the organic, handheld content people trust on TikTok and Instagram, but it's generated from your product photos, a script, and an AI voice.

How is this different from hiring a real UGC creator?

A real creator needs shipping time, filming time, and reshoots if something's off. An AI UGC ad skips all of that — no product to mail, no shoot day to schedule, and revisions happen inside the generation pipeline instead of a second filming session.

What do you need from me to get started?

A handful of clear product photos, a short brief on who the ad is for and what it should say, and any brand guidelines or past ads you'd like it to match. No script needed — writing the hook and structure is part of the process.

Which AI tools do you use?

Veo3, Kling, Runway, Higgsfield, Seedance, Nano Banana, and Midjourney for visuals and avatars, ElevenLabs for voice, and CapCut for final editing and pacing.

Can the AI avatar look like an actual spokesperson for my brand?

Yes. An avatar can be built to represent a consistent brand face across multiple ads, or generated fresh for each campaign if you'd rather rotate looks.

What formats and aspect ratios do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts by default, with square 1:1 and landscape 16:9 available for feed ads, YouTube, or paid media.

How long does one ad take to produce?

Most single ads are delivered within a few days of receiving the brief and photos, depending on length and how many concepts you want tested. Ongoing content runs on a weekly or biweekly schedule instead.

Do I need to write the script?

No — the hook, structure, and pacing are written from scratch, drawing on a background in mass communication and journalism. You're also welcome to send your own script or talking points.

What does a project cost?

Rates start from an hourly baseline listed on Upwork, with most single ads quoted as a flat project fee once the brief, length, and number of concepts are clear.

Can you make ads in languages other than English?

Yes — English, Yoruba, and French are all native or fluent, so scripts, voiceover, and on-screen copy can be produced directly in any of the three.

Do you take on ongoing content, like a weekly channel?

Yes — recurring narrative and UGC channels are already part of the regular workload, including long-form YouTube storytelling and short-form TikTok series on a fixed delivery schedule.
